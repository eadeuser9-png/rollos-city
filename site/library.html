Owner: upload a .txt or .pdf book

Not limited to Project Gutenberg. Add your own writing, public-domain books, or works you have permission to store. You can upload UTF-8 .txt files (1.5 MB maximum) or text-based .pdf files (20 MB maximum). PDFs are converted to selectable text on your device, then only the extracted text (1.5 MB maximum) is stored in Cloudflare. Image-only/scanned PDFs must first be converted using OCR. Book titles, brief excerpts, and reading notes may appear publicly, so do not upload private or confidential text.

Owner: add a Project Gutenberg book

Download the plain-text .txt file yourself from a Gutenberg book's page, then import it here. The book is available to Rollo after saving. Your owner token stays in this page only and is never saved.

Rollo's reading history

A record of completed reading visits, actual passages, where he stopped, and any AI-generated takeaways supported by exact quotations. Without AI, progress still saves but takeaways are not invented. Separate from his journal.
